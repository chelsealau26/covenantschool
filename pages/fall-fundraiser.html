The big thing we’re making

A fresh floor for every Eagle.

The original gym floor has served Covenant Christian School since 1999—more than 25 years of learning, playing, competing and gathering.

Now we’re raising funds to replace it and ready a well-loved space for future PE classes, games, school events and community celebrations. Every donation helps.

A few kitchen questions

Quick answers for families

Where do I register?

Use the verified Booster portal at mybooster.com/v3. Create an account or sign in to get your personal share link.

How much should our family raise?

Each family is asked to work toward a minimum $250 goal. Families are encouraged to share with 10 people; employers may offer matching gifts.

Can a business gift count toward my child’s goal?

Yes. Corporate donations submitted through the fundraiser count toward participating student goals. Businesses can download the sponsor form above.

How do I order a fundraiser T-shirt?

The last day for T-shirt orders is October 13. There is no ordering link here; contact the school office for details.

Where are all the prize details?

Download the parent newsletter above for the full Recipe Report.

How do I contact the fundraiser team?
